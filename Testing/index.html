About the IT Skills Olympics

The IT Skills Olympics is an event that brings together students from different schools to showcase their knowledge, creativity, and technical skills in information technology. Through various competitions, participants can demonstrate their abilities, exchange ideas, and explore new opportunities in the digital world.

Competitions

Explore the different competitions featured in the 15th IT Skills Olympics.

Application Development Using Android Programming

Showcase your creativity and technical skills by developing mobile applications. Participants can explore different categories, including IoT, games, and productivity applications.

Cybersecurity

Demonstrate your knowledge of digital security, threat awareness, and information protection. Participants will be challenged to apply their skills in identifying and addressing cybersecurity concerns.

Database Programming

Test your ability to organize, manage, and manipulate data using database technologies. Participants will demonstrate their understanding of database design and programming.

Digital Electronics

Apply your understanding of electronic circuits, digital logic, and technical components. This competition highlights practical knowledge and problem-solving skills in electronics.

Java Programming

Show your programming abilities through Java-based coding challenges. Participants will demonstrate their understanding of programming concepts, logic, and problem-solving.

Python Programming

Demonstrate your knowledge of Python through coding exercises and programming challenges. Participants will use logical thinking and creativity to develop solutions.

Computer Networking

Showcase your understanding of network systems, connectivity, and infrastructure. Participants will apply their technical knowledge to networking-related challenges.

Linux Administration

Demonstrate your ability to work with Linux operating systems and system administration. Participants will showcase their knowledge of commands, configurations, and system management.

Web Design

Unleash your creativity by designing and developing attractive and functional websites. Participants will demonstrate their skills in web technologies, layout, and user experience.

IT Quiz Bee

Put your information technology knowledge to the test through an exciting academic competition. Participants will answer questions covering various IT concepts and technologies.

E-Sports: Mobile Legends Bang Bang

Experience competitive gaming through teamwork, strategy, and coordination. Participants will compete in Mobile Legends: Bang Bang, showcasing their skills and game knowledge.

E-Sports: Call of Duty Mobile

Showcase your teamwork, communication, and strategic thinking in competitive gaming. Participants will compete in Call of Duty: Mobile and demonstrate their coordination and game skills.
