About the IT Skills Olympics

The IT Skills Olympics is an event that brings together students from different schools to showcase their knowledge, creativity, and technical skills in information technology. Through various competitions, participants can demonstrate their abilities, exchange ideas, and explore new opportunities in the digital world.

Competitions

Explore the different competitions featured in the 15th IT Skills Olympics.

Application Development Using Android Programming

Develop and present Android applications under the categories of Game, Productivity, and Internet of Things (IoT). Participants will showcase their creativity and technical skills in creating applications for Android devices.

Cybersecurity

Test your cybersecurity skills through Capture the Flag challenges using Kali Linux. Participants will solve security-related puzzles involving security principles, network setup, endpoint protection, and vulnerability assessment.

Database Programming

Demonstrate your SQL knowledge by solving database-related problems using basic to advanced queries. Participants will showcase their logical thinking, analytical skills, and ability to develop efficient solutions.

Digital Electronics

Showcase your knowledge of Boolean algebra and logical expression simplification. Participants will apply Boolean techniques to design efficient digital circuits and demonstrate their understanding of digital electronics.

Java Programming

Demonstrate your Java programming skills by solving algorithmic challenges within a given time limit. Participants will showcase their coding proficiency, debugging abilities, logical thinking, and problem-solving skills.

Python Programming

Test your Python programming skills through algorithmic challenges that require efficient coding and problem-solving. Participants will demonstrate their knowledge of Python syntax, debugging, and ability to develop creative solutions.

Computer Networking

Apply your networking knowledge through practical challenges using Cisco Packet Tracer. Participants will configure network devices, implement network topologies, and troubleshoot connectivity and network-related problems.

Linux Administration

Demonstrate your Linux system administration skills through practical technical challenges. Participants will perform tasks involving system configuration, user management, file permissions, networking, security, and system maintenance.

Web Design

Design and develop responsive, functional, and visually appealing websites using HTML and CSS. Participants will showcase their creativity and technical skills while applying user interface (UI) and user experience (UX) principles.

IT Quiz Bee

Test your knowledge of Information and Communications Technology through questions covering computer systems, programming, networking, cybersecurity, databases, software engineering, and other IT-related topics.

E-Sports: Mobile Legends Bang Bang

Compete in Mobile Legends: Bang Bang through teamwork, strategy, and coordination. Participants will develop sportsmanship, leadership, communication, and camaraderie while showcasing their competitive gaming skills.

E-Sports: Call of Duty Mobile

Showcase your competitive gaming abilities through teamwork, communication, and strategic coordination. Participants will demonstrate sportsmanship, leadership, and cooperation while competing in Call of Duty: Mobile.
